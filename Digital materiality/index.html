Every class in Creative Technology pushes me outside my comfort zone, but that's a good thing, because learning new things is exactly why I'm here.

TouchDesigner operates on a completely different logic from the Adobe Suite and Figma I'm used to. It felt like learning an entirely new language.

Building on the in-class project, I just started tinkering — tweaking parameters here and there, and ended up with a lot of unexpected effects. Pretty cool.

The most memorable moment was watching a classmate use KantanMapper to project visuals onto individual sheets of A4 paper pinned on the wall. That was genuinely incredible.
