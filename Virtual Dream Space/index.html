Soft City

Inspired by Mexican architect Barragán, the game Monument Valley, and the subtle colors of translucent materials, I wanted to build a city that is richly colored and soft in texture.

In Blender, I first created various buildings as modular components for assembling the city, and applied gradient color materials to them.

Problems arose when importing the models from Blender into Unity — all materials were lost. I tried various settings and file formats, including .blend and .fbx files, but the models still appeared without materials in Unity. I then attempted to re-apply gradient materials directly in Unity, but the results were mediocre. In the end, I decided to use flat solid colors instead.

Explore in colorful city, fly and walk.

In the game, I built a flying carpet that the player can ride to reach elevated areas and explore the city. Although my Virtual Dream City wasn't quite as "soft" as I envisioned, it did, in a way, fulfill my fantasy of wandering through building blocks and architectural forms.
